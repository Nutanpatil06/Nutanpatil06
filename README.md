# About Me

I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate driven by a curiosity for how artificial intelligence can transform healthcare and medicine. My work focuses on building AI systems that help doctors make better decisions, detect diseases earlier, and deliver personalized care to patients.

My interest in Medical AI grew from watching family members manage chronic conditions with limited tools. I realized that technology could do more. This led me to explore how machine learning, deep learning, and large language models can be applied to biomedical problems. Through hands-on projects, I have worked on systems that connect traditional medicine knowledge with modern AI, and tools that help manage health data in meaningful ways.

I am now pursuing graduate studies in Biomedical Science to deepen my understanding of physiology, genomics, and clinical research. My goal is to become a researcher who builds AI tools grounded in real medical science and designed to serve patients.
